02 · Colour

Warm paper, one accent, colour reserved for sign

Dark ink on light stock throughout. Green and red appear only on signed values — never as decoration, never as a mood. The burnt orange is the single accent and it is spent in five places: the 9px bar across the top of every slide, the eyebrow, the index number, the rail on the interpretation slide, and one highlighted bar in a chart. Nothing else on the account is orange.

--paper#F4EFE3

Ground. Warm enough to read as stock rather than as UI.

--ink#12100B

Text and every chart stroke. Not pure black — 12/16/11 keeps it printed, not screen-lit.

--acc#B2560F

The one accent.

Five uses, listed above. A sixth means the system is drifting.

--pos#0F6236

Positive values only.

--neg#A11F13

Negative values only.

--rule#C6BCA6

Hairlines. Content hangs off these instead of sitting in cards.

--dim#69614F

Sources, axis labels, timestamps.

--dim2#38332A

Standfirsts and pull quotes — softer than ink, not grey.

Texture, not gradient: a 54px graph-paper grid printed at 5.5% ink, a 4px double rule under the masthead, a 2.5px solid rule above the footnote.

One rule that looks like a detail and is not: mono is for tabular columns and standalone figures only. Setting it on numbers inside running text rendered 2.1% as 2 . 1%, because the isolate inherits the face. Inline numbers use font-family:inherit.

And the hero figure is sized to its measure, not to a constant: min(256, 940 / (len × 0.62)). At the four-character size, 1,100% ran its percent sign off the right edge of the frame.

06 · Hebrew

Right-to-left is where the bugs live

The slide is direction:rtl and the Unicode bidi algorithm reorders anything left unisolated. Four rules, each of which cost a published mistake.

The maqaf serves two masters

U+05BE (־) is both a prefix hyphen and a minus sign in this channel. כ־5% means "about 5%"; (־2.62%) means minus. A leading hyphen is a minus only when nothing word-like precedes it.

const PCT = /(?<![\w.,])((?:(?<![֐-׿])[+\-−־])?\d+(?:[.,]\d+)?%)/gu;

Without the inner lookbehind, six figures on one deck flipped sign. Without [.,] in the outer one, (־2.62%) matched as 62%.

Isolate ranges before numbers

// whole range first, as ONE span — a dash between two isolates
// takes the paragraph direction and renders 23:00–23:15 backwards
.replace(/(?<![\w.])(\d{1,2}[:.]\d{2}\s*[–—\-־]\s*\d{1,2}[:.]\d{2})/gu, NUM)
// then one isolate per NUMBER — never per digit group, or 14/05 renders 05/14.
// Currency rides inside the isolate, or $325 renders 325$.
.replace(/(?<![\w.])([$€£₪]?[+\-−]?\d+(?:[.,:\/]\d+)*%?)/gu, NUM)

Bidi leaks into SVG

direction:rtl inherits into SVG <text>. Under RTL the sign and percent glyph of +7.30% reorder out of the run and the label lands on top of its own bar. Every chart label is numeric or Latin, so the chart is pinned LTR.

.ch{direction:ltr} .ch text{direction:ltr;unicode-bidi:isolate}

Load order, and a vocabulary mismatch

Structure first, skin second — reversed, every skin override loses at equal specificity and the slide renders as empty boxes. And colour both vocabularies: the archetype emits up/dn while charts use pos/neg. A skin matching only one rendered every signed hero figure in plain ink for a week.

07 · Not this

What "too Claude-made" meant

The brief that produced this system was a rejection. These are the specific things that were wrong, kept as a list because each one is tempting again every time the deck is touched.

Rounded cards

Content hangs off hairlines on a grid. A card is a way of avoiding a decision about hierarchy.

Soft shadows

Paper does not glow. Everything is flat ink on flat stock.

Centred everything

The body is optically centred; text is not. A cover hangs off the top rule.

Pastel wash

One ground, one ink, one accent. A gradient is a stand-in for a typographic idea.

Emoji as graphic

One leading decorative emoji is stripped from every slide string. They stay in the caption.

Body text on slides

Slides carry headlines and figures. The reporting lives in the caption, where it is searchable.

Invented numbers

Every figure comes from the channel or from arithmetic on channel figures — and the chart note says which.

A shrinking font floor

When content overflows, the fix is fewer rows. The build fails rather than clipping a slide.
